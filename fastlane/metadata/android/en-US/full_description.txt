Comprehensive mobile application developed with Flutter for administrative management and interactive utility tools.

Key features:
- Authentication & User Registration: Secure sign-in with email and password.
- Clients Module: Real-time client creation, update, listing, and deletion.
- Classifications Module: Categorized information management.
- PokéAPI Explorer: Interactive lookup of Pokémon data via REST API.
- Phone Contacts Directory: Read device contacts for direct WhatsApp message sending.
- Barcode & QR Scanner: Multi-format barcode and QR code reader with support for institutional URLs.
